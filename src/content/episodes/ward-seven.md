We recorded with Priya Okonkwo-Hale in the ward's small kitchen during her break, which started at 04:20 and lasted, in the end, eleven minutes. She picked the conversation up again after a call bell, and then again after another.

She talks about the hour before dawn when everyone, patients and staff, is at their lowest, how she writes handover notes so the day team can see the night that happened, and the questions people only ask in the dark.

## In this episode

- How a night shift is planned, and how it actually goes
- Sleep, food and family on a rota that changes every week
- The small rituals that hold a night team together
