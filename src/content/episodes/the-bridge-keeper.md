The Halden Street bridge opens about nine times a night. Each lift takes four minutes, and Desmond Achterberg has done roughly 31,000 of them.

From the operator's cabin, with the river black below and the city's lights doubled in it, he walks us through a lift from the first radio call to the last gate going up. He has opinions about cyclists who race the bells, a soft spot for the tugboat crews, and one story about a swan that he tells better than we can summarize.

## In this episode

- What a bridge operator watches for, and why it's mostly weather
- The radio channel that the river uses, and its manners
- Why the night shift gets the heavy barges
- The swan
