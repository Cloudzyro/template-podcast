Before Night Ferry was a podcast it was a ferry. The Harbor Line's last crossing leaves at 01:15, and for six winters Teo Lind tied it up on the far side.

In this first episode Odalys rides with him, and they work out what the show is going to be: a weekly visit to someone who works while the rest of the city sleeps.
