Odalys produced the overnight show at Harbor FM for nine years. For this episode she went back to studio B to interview the host she used to produce for, Inez Calloway, between songs and calls on a live show.

They talk about the people who call at 2 a.m., the strange intimacy of talking to a city that's mostly asleep, and the song Inez plays at five every morning.
